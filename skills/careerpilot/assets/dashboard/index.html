Primary portals and application rules

The primary search creates a saved request for your connected AI/browser. Copy its prompt into your agent chat to run it. The dashboard does not directly scrape LinkedIn or Indeed. Each configured source must be checked; blocked access is reported, never replaced silently by another source.

Exclude LinkedIn Easy Apply and Indeed Apply / Easily apply / Apply with Indeed. A posting enters the primary list only after the agent verifies the employer or ATS destination and confirms no on-platform application option. Unverified routes are excluded pending verification. All qualifying matches remain ranked, including low scores.

Supplementary search runs Remotive, Jobicy and Arbeitnow public feeds directly. Those results are separate and do not satisfy the required primary search or external-application verification. Remotive is delayed by 24 hours and cached 6 hours. Jobicy covers the last 7 days. Jobicy and Arbeitnow are cached 1 hour and capped at 5 pages each. Missing posting dates stay unknown.
